Rabbitplace is a site im building for my own enjoyment mostly, to collect my creations, my thoughts, and things I enjoy. Front end /css coding has been a hobby of mine for a long time! Home: my home page, where you can find a little bit of everything!
Bedroom: my personal space, where you can learn about me, read my blog and look at my kitty or clothes
Library: Here i list media that i do and do not enjoy, and collect article and writing of my own!
Gallery: a place for showcasing my illustrations and ocs, and information if you want to commission me to draw something!
study: Hobby corner! I have a lot of hobbies i cycle through! These pages include showcases of things ive made and resources i find useful
Garden: A place for pretty things and collections! Cliques, clubs, shrines and all that sorta stuff!
Storage: Sitemap, credits, coding resources, unsorted stuff etc goes here!

some pages will connect and link to each other, hopefully that'll make it easier to find the things you're actually interested in!
